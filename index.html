An Overview of a Few Topics You Can Learn

Building, Programming, and Notebooking are pretty broad areas in Vex Robotics.
Here are some of the major topics you can learn as a beginner on this web page.

  • Designing a robot

    The first step in any season is to brainstorm and design a solution to the problem. Modeling structures and their unique capabilties is essential. Learn about that process here.

  • Having proper build quality

    Having build quality is essential for any time to ensure their success in competition. Learn how to efficiently build, secure, and wire robots.

  • Coding Driver Control

    Without a functioning driver control, no robotics team can possibly play in the Vex Robotics Competition. Learn how to custom fit driver control for your team.

  • Coding Autonomous Functions

    A good autonomous can not only help you secure higher rankings in competition, but also higher rankings in the worlds skills rankings. Learn how to code a robot to execute tasks itself.

  • Documenting Organization

    Proper organization and a table of contents in a team's engineering notebook helps judges immensley in tracking progress over time. Learn how to effectively organize your documentation.

  • Rubric Analysis

    Effecitvely analyzing the awards notebook rubric is essential to become competitive in the judging categoy. Learn what judges are looking for in a strong engineering notebook.
